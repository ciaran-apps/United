Sources & notes

Squad, results and this season

  • Squad, results and cup runs: the club's 2026/27 season record, ESPN, BBC Sport and match reports.
  • This season's appearances and goals: unitedstats.co.uk. Premier League minutes and cards: VAVEL.
  • Updated every Monday morning; the date is in the header.

Player history

  • Every player to appear for United since 2013/14, all competitions, from each season's squad statistics in the club's Wikipedia season records.
  • Assists: 2013/14 to 2019/20 from FBref (its early seasons leave out a few domestic cup games); 2020/21 onwards from United In Focus's season assist tables. Sources count assists slightly differently.

Transfers and money

  • All money is in pounds, using reported base fees; add-ons are noted but not counted.
  • The deal lists cover major first-team moves, not every academy transfer, so spend, sales and net spend leave out add-ons and small undisclosed fees.
  • Summer departures count for the manager in charge of that transfer window.
  • Season views put each deal in the window it happened: June–December into that season, January–May into the one already running.

Player wages

  • All player wages, current and past, are Capology estimates from screenshots of every season from 2013/14 to 2026/27. Each season was checked against Capology's total. A player Capology doesn't list counts as £0.
  • One manual correction: Jadon Sancho is set to £250k a week for every season at the club (2021/22 to 2025/26). Capology shows less for his loan years because the loan clubs paid part of it.

Contracts

  • Contract end dates for the current squad are from Capology (checked 31 Oct 2026). All run to 30 June of the year shown. Red means the contract ends within a year.
  • Tynan Thompson isn't listed on Capology yet, so he has no date.

Wage bill

  • Total staff costs from United's annual accounts (year to 30 June): 2015/16, 2018/19, 2019/20 and 2020/21 to 2025/26 checked against published reports; earlier years from the club's accounts as widely reported.

Managers

  • Games, wins, draws and losses: Wikipedia's list of United managers, with Carrick's updated after each game.

Badges and links

  • Badges show initials until real photos are added. The premierleague.com profile links use player codes from FPL Core Insights.
